Understanding Your Plan Documents

Deductible

The amount you pay for covered health care services before your insurance plan starts to pay.

Co-pay

A fixed amount you pay for a covered health care service, usually when you receive the service.

Co-insurance

Your share of the costs of a covered service, calculated as a percentage of the allowed amount.

Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The most you pay during a policy period before your insurance pays 100% of the allowed amount.

MindCare

Connect with licensed therapists and counselors for confidential mental health support. Your account is pre-registered and ready to use.

Getting started:

  1. Claim your pre-registered account with full name and DOB
  2. Complete a brief intake questionnaire
